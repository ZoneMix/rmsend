A Little Room to Read

A sample of rmsend's e-ink reading layout

The rmsend project

A page made for paper

Good reading needs very little: clear words, comfortable spacing, and a place to pause. This sample uses sturdy serif type, black ink, and a wide margin for your own thoughts.

Articles arrive without navigation bars or subscription buttons. Headings, emphasis, links, figures, and notes keep their place in the text.

Leave a little room for the reader.

— A note from the project

The margin belongs to you

Underline a useful sentence. Ask a question. Make a connection. The right edge stays open for a stylus, while the text fits the reMarkable 2 screen.

For a different reading experience, export EPUB and choose your preferred font and size on the tablet.
